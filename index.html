About this atlas & sources

An original, schematic interpretation of northwestern Middle-earth. Heights are procedural and exaggerated; positions and routes are approximate. Routes show selected milestones on the outward quests, not return journeys. Underground passages are drawn on the surface. Earlier battles are shown on a late Third Age landscape.

Geography: Wikipedia · Tolkien Gateway maps. Stories: J. R. R. Tolkien’s The Hobbit and The Lord of the Rings. Each entry includes further reading. Unofficial fan project.
